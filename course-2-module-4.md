# Playbook

## Definition
- A manual that provides details about any operational action
- Clarify what tools are needed to respond to a security incident, helping with handling urgent siutations efficently in a prescribed and concise way.
- Playbooks are accompanied by a strategy which outlines the expectations for individuals responsible.
- The plan dictates how the specific task outlined in the playbook must be completed.

## Playbooks cont.
- Playbooks should be treated as a living document with constant change in the industry.
- Updates are made often if
    - A failure is identified
    - There is a change in industry standard
    - The cybersecurity landscape changes due to evolving threat actor tactics
 
## Incident Response Playbook

### Definition
An organizations quick attempt to identify an attack, contain the damage, and correct the effects of a security breach

#### Preparation
- Organizations must prepare the likilihood and risk of a security incident, documenting procedures, establishing staffing plans, and educating users. This is the foundation for successful incident response

#### Detection and Analysis
- Detect and analyze events using the proper tools and strategies, to determine if a breach has occured and determine the magnittude of the incident.

#### Containment
- The goal is to prevent damage, and reduce the immediate impact of a security incident. Security professionals take actions to contain an incident and minimize damage.

#### Eradication and Recovery
- The complete removal of incidents artifacts so that the organization can return to normal operations. Security professionals eliminate artifiacts of the incident by removing malicious code, and mitigating vulnerabilities.

#### Post-Incident Activity
- Documenting the incident, informing organizational leadership, and applying lessons learned so the organization is better prepared to handle future incidents. This includes determining the root cause, and implementing updates or improvements to enhance overall security posture.

#### Coordination
- The final phase in an incident response playbook is coordination, it involves reporting incidents and sharing information throughout the incident response process, based on the organization's standards. It ensures that organizations meet compliance requirements and it allows for coordinated response and solutions.

#### SIEM tools correlation
SIEM tools detect threats and generate alerts, once the alert is detected, the playbook is utilized. Playbooks and SIEM tools go hand in hand.

# Key Takeaways
- Playbooks provide a consistent process for security professionals to follow to address new threats and vulnerabilities. 
- It is essential to refine processes and procedures outlined in a playbook. With every documented incident, teams need to consider what was learned from the incident and what improvements should be made to handle incidents effectively in the future, while taking in account compliance and standards.
- SIEM tools detect threats and generate alerts, once the alert is detected the aforementioned playbook is utilized to best handle the security incident. Playbooks and SIEM tools go hand in hand.
- Playbooks should be treated as a living document with constant change in industry.
    - Updates are made often if
      - A failure is identified
      - There is a change in industry standard
      - The cybersecurity landscape changes due to evolving threat actor tactics
