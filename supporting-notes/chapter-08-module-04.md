## Topics Covered

## Study Notes

Use sources such as OWASP to review common application risks. Industry publications and practitioner accounts can help track developments and see how security teams investigate and respond. Campus groups such as CISO Club give you a place to discuss defensive projects, ask for feedback, and explain what you built.

## Key Takeaways

- Verify important claims against primary sources before applying them.
- Bring a specific question or a short project write-up to community discussions.
- Keep a record of ideas you can test or incorporate into your portfolio.
