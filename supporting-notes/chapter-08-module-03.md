- Evidence-based incident updates
- Visualizing phishing exercise results

## Study Notes

Tailor each update to the decisions its audience needs to make. An incident ticket can carry technical details and a timeline, while a leadership brief can focus on impact, options, and a decision request. For a phishing exercise, use a bar chart by department and include the number of recipients and reporting period so readers can compare click rates and counts.

## Key Takeaways

- State the issue, business impact, and requested action up front.
- Include enough evidence for the decision and identify any uncertainties.
- Choose the communication channel based on urgency and audience.
