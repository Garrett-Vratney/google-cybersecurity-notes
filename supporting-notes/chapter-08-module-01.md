## Study Notes

Identify business-critical assets and classify them by the harm that loss, disclosure, or disruption could cause. Apply the organization’s rules for access, storage, sharing, retention, and disposal. A business continuity plan keeps essential operations running during disruption. A disaster recovery plan focuses on restoring systems, infrastructure, and data. Exercise both plans and maintain current contacts and steps.

## Key Takeaways

- Classify data early so protection follows it throughout its lifecycle.
- Escalate based on asset criticality, suspected exposure, and business impact.
- Test restoration and continuity procedures before an incident.
