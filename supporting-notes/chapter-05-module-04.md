## Study Notes

Phishing can arrive by email, text, phone, or social media. Verify unexpected requests through a known channel. Viruses attach to host files, worms spread independently, and trojans disguise themselves as legitimate software. Use parameterized queries for SQL injection prevention and context-appropriate output encoding for XSS prevention. Threat modeling starts with assets, data flows, and trust boundaries, then identifies and ranks potential failures.

## Key Takeaways

- Treat urgency and unusual requests as reasons to verify identity and intent.
- Choose controls for the specific weakness and test their effect.
- Use STRIDE to categorize threats and PASTA to connect them to business risk.
