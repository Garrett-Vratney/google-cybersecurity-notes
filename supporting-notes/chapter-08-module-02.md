## Study Notes

Classify suspected incidents by their nature, scope, urgency, and potential business impact. Malware infection, unauthorized access, and suspected data exposure can require different responders. Follow the incident playbook to contact the responsible team. A data controller determines why and how personal data is processed, a processor carries out processing on its behalf, and a custodian implements operational controls for assigned data.

## Key Takeaways

- Escalate promptly when severity, ownership, or response authority requires it.
- Include timestamps, affected assets, supporting evidence, and actions already taken.
- Label suspected impact and confirmed findings clearly.
