## Topics Covered

- TCP/IP and OSI models
- SIEM fundamentals
- Behavioral interview answers using STAR

## Study Notes

Practice explaining TCP/IP as a four-layer model and OSI as a seven-layer reference model. Trace a familiar connection through application, transport, IP, and local link layers. A SIEM collects, normalizes, searches, and correlates events so analysts can detect patterns and investigate alerts. Use STAR for behavioral questions by describing the situation, task, action, and result. A useful example could cover a file-share permissions issue from your Proxmox project, using the actual outcome.

## Key Takeaways

- Explain each concept with a concrete example.
- State your specific actions and how you verified the result.
- Prepare both technical explanations and behavioral stories.
