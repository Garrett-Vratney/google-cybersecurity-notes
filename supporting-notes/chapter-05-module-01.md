## Study Notes

Maintain an inventory of assets and record ownership, sensitivity, and business importance. A threat can exploit a weakness and cause harm to an asset. Policies set direction, standards specify requirements, and procedures give execution steps. IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS distribute operational responsibilities differently. The source uses the five-function NIST CSF model. CSF 2.0 adds Govern alongside Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.

## Key Takeaways

- Use classification and business impact to prioritize controls.
- Define who owns each asset and which party secures each cloud component.
- Use the CSF to organize outcomes and spot gaps in the security program.
