## Study Notes

A security event is an observable occurrence. Analysts assess whether an event threatens confidentiality, integrity, or availability and meets incident criteria. A CSIRT coordinates investigation and response, while a SOC monitors and triages activity. IDS generates alerts, EDR supplies endpoint telemetry and response capabilities, SIEM centralizes and analyzes logs, and SOAR coordinates repeatable workflows. A handler journal records time, observations, evidence, actions, and decisions.

## Key Takeaways

- An alert is a starting point for validation.
- Track false positives and false negatives when evaluating detection coverage.
- Document who owns decisions, evidence collection, and escalation.
