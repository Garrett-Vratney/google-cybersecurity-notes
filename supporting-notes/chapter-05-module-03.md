## Study Notes

Inventory assets, find weaknesses, prioritize by impact and exposure, assign remediation, and verify fixes. CVE identifies a disclosed vulnerability, while CVSS describes technical severity. Internal and external scans show different views of exposure. Credentialed scans can inspect patch levels and configurations more closely. Defensive pipelines can check dependencies, enforce least privilege, and review releases.

## Key Takeaways

- Use asset criticality and exposure alongside CVSS to prioritize remediation.
- Track end-of-life software and verify that fixes actually removed the weakness.
- Use MFA, rate limits, and authentication monitoring to reduce credential abuse.
