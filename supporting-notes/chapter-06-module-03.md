## Study Notes

Correlate alerts with endpoint, network, identity, and pipeline evidence before deciding whether an event is an incident. Watch for unusual pipeline triggers, configuration changes, or release activity. Record who collected evidence, when it was collected, and every transfer. Contain the affected scope, remove the cause, and restore and monitor services. Document the timeline, impact, and follow-up actions.

## Key Takeaways

- Prioritize validated incidents by business impact and urgency.
- Record evidence handling as it happens.
- Assign owners and due dates for improvements from the incident review.
